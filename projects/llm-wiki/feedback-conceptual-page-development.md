When revising or building a conceptual/architecture wiki page (as opposed to ingesting a new external source), Daren works through the idea incrementally across multiple turns — restating a concept, then correcting or adding a nuance, then adding another — rather than handing over a finished framing in one message. Each turn should get a tight synthesis back (what it maps onto in the existing page, at most one clarifying question) rather than a full section rewrite in response.

**Why:** Demonstrated across a 2026-09-16 session reworking [[network-segmentation-model]] (llm-wiki): the two-lineage framing (engineering blast-radius containment vs. security threat containment) arrived over four separate turns, each confirmed or redirected before the next — dropping the propagation-zone tier out to its own page, keeping "policy zone" (zone-based firewall, DMZ, VRF-to-VRF) distinct from microsegmentation rather than collapsing them, naming the DMZ as security's one purpose-built segment, then adding the IP-subnet/gateway refinement. Each landed as a small, targeted edit to the already-rewritten page, not a renewed full draft.

**How to apply:** In conceptual-page sessions, resist drafting prose after the first explanation — reflect the idea back, map it onto what already exists in the page/wiki, and ask at most one sharpening question. Once the user gives a clearly decisive structure (an explicit "draft it," or an instruction confident enough to act on), write the full page in one pass — the Write tool is fine for a near-total rewrite at that point. After that initial rewrite, treat further additions from the user as targeted Edits to specific sections, not further full rewrites. Related: [[feedback-wiki-writing-style]].

When a concept originates from Daren's own raw personal notes (audio stream-of-consciousness, handwritten, typed) rather than from an external source, the development order is: interrogate Daren first to extract as much of his own perspective and detail as possible, and only afterward augment with external industry/market research — never the reverse.

**Why:** Raised 2026-11-27 during a conversation about building a "research a concept → wiki ingest" subagent — the candidate pattern had been research-first. Daren pointed out that the concepts which actually got expanded into rich wiki pages (AIM, continuous discovery, network digital twin systems thinking, the segmentation model, etc.) all trace back to his own original thinking captured in raw notes, not to an external source needing synthesis. Leading with external research risks overwriting or diluting his own framing before it has been fully drawn out — external material's job is to augment and contextualize a perspective that's already been extracted, not to lead it.

**How to apply:** When a new idea shows up as an inbox note, audio transcript, or handwritten/typed capture that reads as Daren's own thinking (as distinct from a link to someone else's article, paper, or podcast), lead with clarifying questions that pull out more of his own detail and nuance before reaching for web search or any external source. Treat external research as a second pass, run once his own framing is as complete as he can make it in conversation — this is the opposite order from a normal source ingest, where the external material comes first and wiki synthesis follows.

**Implemented 2026-11-27** as an explicit classification step, not just a conversational norm: `llm-wiki/.claude/commands/process-inbox.md` (live sessions) classifies each note before merging/drafting and switches to interrogation if it reads as original thinking, leaving the note in `inbox/` until a page is actually drafted. The unattended `remarkable-wiki-ingest` scheduled task (`d:\Users\daren\.claude\scheduled-tasks\remarkable-wiki-ingest\SKILL.md`, Step 2.6) can't interrogate Daren, so it defers instead — leaves the note unprocessed and flags it in the daily summary email as "awaiting discussion" rather than auto-ingesting it. Default to the "original thinking" classification when ambiguous in both cases: a wrongly-deferred note just waits a bit longer; a wrongly-ingested one risks overwriting Daren's own framing before it's fully drawn out.
